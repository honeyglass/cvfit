# Senior Backend Engineer at go-e Labs, Berlin

A summary in our own words of a real posting, read on 2026-11-16 on the job board of the German
Federal Employment Agency: [reference 10001-1003921339-S](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/jobdetail/10001-1003921339-S).
Full time, permanent, Berlin or Graz, posted in English.

**Tasks**

- Scale the Go and TypeScript services of an IoT platform for electric-car chargers, at about
  250,000 requests per second, with ClickHouse and PostgreSQL behind them
- Turn the existing APIs into one platform with contract-first design (OpenAPI, gRPC, GraphQL)
- Own backend security: identity, OAuth2/OIDC, fine-grained access and Zero Trust
- Design a white-label telemetry platform for customers with strict data residency
- Lead architecture decisions and coach other senior engineers

**Must have**

- Senior engineer who has driven architecture across several teams
- Distributed security models, API design with backward compatibility, multi-tenant platforms
- Expert Go and TypeScript in high-concurrency, event-driven systems
- ClickHouse or similar OLAP stores and PostgreSQL at scale

**Nice to have**

- IoT protocols such as OCPP or MQTT, edge and hybrid deployments, domain-driven design, Rust,
  C++ or Zig
