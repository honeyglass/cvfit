# Cook at GALERIA Restaurant, Munich

A summary in our own words of a real posting, read on 2026-11-16 on the job board of the German
Federal Employment Agency: [reference 10001-1003921439-S](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/jobdetail/10001-1003921439-S).
Full time or part time, permanent, a restaurant in the center of Munich, posted in German.

**Tasks**

- Prepare dishes to recipes and standards
- Arrange dishes at the buffet
- Keep service and production running smoothly
- Prepare and wrap up the daily business
- Receive goods and store food
- Follow hygiene rules

**Must have**

- First experience as a cook
- Initiative, teamwork, openness to new things
- Focus on guests and service
- Reliability and a talent for organisation
- A friendly manner and good manners
