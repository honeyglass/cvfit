# cv/

The commands for your master CVs: create one, list them, and look inside one.

A master CV is the full record of your career: every job, skill and contact line. You keep one
per language, and every version for a job posting starts from it. This folder creates a new,
empty master with a placeholder photo, lists the masters with whether each fits its pages, and
shows the outline of one master or one of its blocks in full.

Changing a master happens in `edit/`; tailoring it to a posting happens in `profile/`.
